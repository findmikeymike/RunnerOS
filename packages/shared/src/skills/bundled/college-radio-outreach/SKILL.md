# College Radio Outreach

Use this skill after `college-radio-matcher` produces directory candidates. Directory data is a lead, not current proof. Verify the station, contact, show, and submission instructions on current public pages before drafting.

## Required inputs

- Artist and release name
- Release type and date
- Genre, vibe, and 2–5 honest sound-alikes
- Clean/explicit status and clean-edit availability
- Hometown and relevant tour markets
- Stream, download, and one-sheet links
- Verified station/contact/show and current submission method
- Why this release fits this specific station

Do not invent missing facts, contacts, show names, quotes, airplay, press, relationships, or audience numbers.

## Verify before drafting

For each target, record:

- evidence URL
- date checked
- station and show status
- named contact, if publicly listed
- required delivery channel: email, form, upload, or physical
- attachment/link rules
- single/album and clean/explicit restrictions
- confidence and unresolved gaps

Label unverified directory rows `directory_only`. Do not call a target verified without current evidence and a timestamp.

## Submission draft

Follow the station's current instructions first. If it accepts email and gives no stricter format:

- Use a plain, searchable subject such as `Music Submission — [Artist] — [Release]`.
- Address a named music director or show host only when verified.
- Keep the message short and specific.
- State the sound, release type, clean/explicit status, and local/tour connection.
- Give one real reason it fits this station or show.
- Include the exact stream/download/one-sheet links the station permits.
- Use one clear, low-pressure ask.
- Never attach files when instructions prohibit attachments.

```markdown
To:
Subject: Music Submission — [Artist] — [Release]

Hi [verified name / station music team],

I'm [artist], a [city] [genre/description] artist. I'm sharing [release] for consideration because [specific verified station/show/local reason].

Stream: [link]
Download: [link, if allowed]
One-sheet: [link]
Clean/explicit: [status]

Thanks for listening,
[sender name and contact]
```

## Delivery rules

- Form or upload portal: draft the field-by-field submission; do not bypass it with email.
- Physical-only: prepare a mailing checklist and label; do not claim anything was mailed.
- Albums-only: do not submit a single as though it qualifies.
- Positive/clean-only: exclude incompatible material or use a verified clean edit.
- No attachments: links only.
- Unknown method: research again; do not guess.

## Follow-up

Follow the station's published cadence. If none exists, prepare one polite follow-up after a reasonable listening window, then stop unless invited to continue. Never pressure on-air staff, manufacture fan requests, or imply airplay is owed.

## Rights language

Songwriters and publishers can register compositions with a performing-rights organization such as ASCAP or BMI for eligible composition performance royalties. Do not imply this pays recording artists or labels a US terrestrial-radio sound-recording royalty; those rights and rules are separate.
