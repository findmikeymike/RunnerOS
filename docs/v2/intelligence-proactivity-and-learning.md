# Intelligence, proactivity, and learning

## Intent

Help Artist OS feel like an attentive working team: it remembers what matters, notices something useful, prepares the next step, and follows through. Support everyday operations, content, advertising and growth alongside distinctive creative opportunities and practical agentic leverage.

These are deferred V2 ideas, not implementation commitments or a major-rewrite plan. Michael asked to preserve them after a current-code assessment. That assessment was not a fresh live-product evaluation.

## Existing foundation

Manager briefs already combine artist context, campaign priorities, release readiness, dated intelligence, growth snapshots and source freshness. Specialist agents, workflows, Calendar, approvals and Outputs provide execution infrastructure. Approved Branding context and memory already exist. Artist Direction, GRAVITY and Builder support creative development and capability construction.

The opportunity is to connect completed work, observed results and the next decision more consistently. Recommendation outcomes currently emphasize completion, approval and execution evidence; those do not by themselves establish artistic or commercial impact.

## 1. Manager review: what changed, what matters

Use existing scheduling and HQ context infrastructure for a finite periodic review. Identify meaningful changes, surface one useful intervention, or stay quiet when nothing warrants attention. Avoid repeating dismissed suggestions or creating work already underway.

Illustrative behavior: “This track is gaining in Chicago. Your next content batch is focused elsewhere. I prepared two locally relevant ideas.” This requires actual comparable evidence; it is not a claim about a current artist.

Begin with internal analysis and drafts under explicit standing permission. Reuse existing approval controls for spending and publishing. Keep source freshness, scope, cadence and cancellation clear.

## 2. Shared knowledge of what works for this artist

Build on existing memory and approved context with a compact, evidence-backed learning record:

- What worked, failed or was rejected, and why.
- Current priorities and practical constraints.
- What is being tested and when to revisit it.

Relevant specialists should retrieve applicable lessons without loading the entire history. Preserve dates, evidence, confidence and scope. Distinguish artist preferences from measured results and provisional hypotheses. Allow correction and supersession; retain approval for enduring artist-identity changes.

## 3. Follow work through to results

For selected work, record its intended result and a review date, then connect later observations to the original Output or activity.

Examples: Did content earn saves? Did an ad bring useful listeners? Did outreach produce a relationship? Keep unavailable measurements explicit and avoid treating correlation as proof of causation.

Feed the findings into the next decision. Reuse existing Outputs and recommendation records where practical, keeping delivery completion separate from evidence of impact.

## 4. Give GRAVITY continuity

GRAVITY already describes dated evidence, opportunity Outputs and retrieval of prior decisions. Make that continuity dependable: revisit one promising opportunity, gather missing evidence, develop a prototype, then continue, change direction or stop.

A finite recurring investigation could let creative work accumulate value over time. Keep ordinary career ownership with Manager and capability construction with Builder. Respect rejected directions, existing work, supported permissions and actual budget controls. Do not imply that a prompt creates an unattended or unlimited execution system.

## Suggested first slice

Pair a concise Manager review with a small shared learning record. First prove that the review notices a meaningful change, uses an existing lesson, prepares a useful next step and stays quiet when appropriate. Extend outcome reviews and GRAVITY recurrence after that behavior is useful in practice.

These notes do not unpark V2 profile enrichment or the separate conversational background-work proposal.
