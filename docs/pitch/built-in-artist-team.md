# Built-In Artist Team

## Landing Page Component

Use a dropdown, accordion, or searchable directory called:

```text
Your Built-In Artist Team
```

Each category should show worker name, one-line job, and a tiny example output.

## Strategy And Command

- **Artist Manager**: routes goals to the right worker, workflow, automation, or setup path.
- **Setup Concierge**: helps connect services, keys, tools, and app setup.
- **World Builder**: builds release worlds, fan experiences, and campaign mechanics.
- **Branding Agent**: builds artist DNA, mythology, positioning, and campaign angles.
- **Legendary Minds**: pressure-tests ideas through strong creative lenses.

## Content And Writing

- **Content Genius**: short-form ideas, hooks, captions, overlays, and content angles.
- **Comms Agent**: fan emails, newsletters, press copy, community updates, and partner messages.
- **Writer**: general drafting and editing.
- **Critic**: structured critique of work in progress.

## Visual And Video

- **Art Director**: single art, album art, merch visuals, visual prompts, and style lanes.
- **Video Editor Agent**: assembles footage, captions, images, and audio into videos.
- **Raw Video Editor**: cuts existing raw footage into clips, reels, shorts, and edits.
- **Hypermotion Agent**: motion graphics, captions, social promos, and render-ready video assets.
- **Lottie Animation Agent**: lightweight web/app animations.

## Outreach And Industry

- **Industry Hunter**: finds A&Rs, labels, managers, publishers, sync people, and connectors.
- **Outreach Agent**: researches people, finds email paths, and drafts high-rapport outreach.
- **Record Doctor**: prepares expert song-review handoff packets.

## Ads And Promotion

- **Ads Strategist**: turns artist context into campaign strategy, audiences, territories, budget logic, and test plans.
- **Ad Creative Agent**: builds hooks, angles, copy, and creative packets for paid campaigns.
- **Ads Agent**: operates Meta/Google ad workflows, diagnostics, reports, browser drafts, and approval-ready changes.
- **IG Music Trending**: prepares inquiry packets for IG music trending providers.
- **Influencer Campaign**: prepares influencer campaign inquiry packets.
- **Playlisting**: prepares playlisting provider inquiry packets.
- **Spotify Analyst**: turns Spotify data into growth signal and campaign context.
- **Spotify Playlist Creator**: plans Spotify playlists around the artist and references.

## Socials

- **Social Publisher**: prepares and executes approved Instagram, TikTok, X, and YouTube actions, including posts, replies, comments, DMs, and multi-profile channel work.
- **TryPost**: prepares TryPost-ready social drafts and schedule packets.
- **YouTube Research Agent**: finds videos, comments, transcripts, channels, and campaign-adjacent YouTube ideas.

## Commerce And Merch

- **Shopify Agent**: product, listing, collection, and store-operation work.
- **Print Agent**: print-on-demand plans, placements, product drafts, QA, and receipts.

## Operations And Research

- **Researcher**: structured research with findings and citations.
- **Triager**: sorts incoming items into urgency and next actions.
- **Orchestrator**: breaks big goals into coordinated worker steps.
- **Coder**: technical work when needed.
- **Update System Agent**: checks installed tools, skills, sources, and maintenance state.

## Copy Block

```text
Artist OS Desktop ships with a real worker roster: strategy, content, visuals, video, outreach, ads, socials, commerce, setup, research, and human handoffs. Open a worker when you know the job. Ask Artist Manager when you do not.
```
