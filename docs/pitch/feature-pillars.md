# Feature Pillars

## 1. Artist HQ

The home base for the artist.

Stores and surfaces the artist profile, voice, branding, assets, network, calendar, community, workers, outputs, and current state of play.

## 2. Campaign Workspaces

Dedicated spaces for releases, rollouts, content sprints, merch drops, ad pushes, and outreach runs.

Campaign workspaces keep the song, world, audience, assets, deadlines, workers, and finals together.

## 3. Specialist Workers

Agents built for music and artist-management work.

They are not generic chat personas. They have job-specific skills, sources, tools, approval rules, and output expectations.

## 4. Living Memory And Shared Intel

Useful facts from chats can become routed workspace intelligence.

The right worker sees the right context without every agent being flooded with every note.

## 5. State Of Play

The app can generate a current operating brief:

- what is happening
- what is ready
- what is blocked
- what needs approval
- what should happen next
- which worker should handle it

## 6. Outputs

Chats are where work happens. Outputs are what the app remembers.

Examples:

- campaign plans
- caption packs
- ad copy
- outreach lists
- visual concepts
- videos
- reports
- receipts

## 7. Finals

Finals are approved or trusted Outputs.

They let artists and managers turn messy exploration into a reusable campaign kit:

- final cover art
- final captions
- final press copy
- final clips
- final artist bio
- final ad angles

## 8. Approval Gates

The app can prepare real work without silently taking risky actions.

Posting, sending, publishing, spending, uploading, deleting, or changing accounts should stay approval-gated.

## 9. Human Layer

Some work should go to humans.

Artist OS Desktop can package the right context and create handoff packets for vetted promo providers, expert song review, and future partner workflows.
