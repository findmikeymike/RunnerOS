# Feature Pillars

## 1. Artist HQ

The home base for the artist.

Stores and surfaces the artist profile, voice, branding, assets, network, calendar, community, workers, outputs, and current state of play.

## 2. Campaign Workspaces

Dedicated spaces for releases, rollouts, content sprints, merch drops, ad pushes, and outreach runs.

Campaign workspaces keep the song, world, audience, assets, deadlines, workers, and finals together.

Pitch angle:

```text
Every release gets its own command center.
```

## 3. Specialist Workers

Agents built for music and artist-management work.

They are not generic chat personas. They have job-specific skills, sources, tools, approval rules, and output expectations.

Landing page idea:

```text
Show a dropdown called "Your Built-In Artist Team" with every included worker grouped by job.
```

## 4. Living Memory And Shared Intel

Useful facts from chats can become routed workspace intelligence.

The right worker sees the right context without every agent being flooded with every note.

This is bigger than generic memory. It is reusable operational context:

- artist profile
- voice
- brand rules
- campaign world
- audience
- approved assets
- prior decisions
- worker-specific intel

## 5. State Of Play

The app can generate a current operating brief:

- what is happening
- what is ready
- what is blocked
- what needs approval
- what should happen next
- which worker should handle it

Pitch angle:

```text
Not "ask me anything." More like "here's the move."
```

## 6. Outputs

Chats are where work happens. Outputs are what the app remembers.

Examples:

- campaign plans
- caption packs
- ad copy
- outreach lists
- visual concepts
- videos
- reports
- receipts

## 7. Finals

Finals are approved or trusted Outputs.

They let artists and managers turn messy exploration into a reusable campaign kit:

- final cover art
- final captions
- final press copy
- final clips
- final artist bio
- final ad angles

This is one of the strongest differences from normal chat tools: the app has a path from idea to durable output to trusted campaign asset.

## 8. Vault / Asset Brain

Artists have messy files. The Vault makes assets legible to both the user and the agents.

Examples:

- masters
- lyrics
- cover art
- press photos
- short-form clips
- references
- logos and marks
- brand assets
- campaign materials

Pitch angle:

```text
Your files stop being a folder. They become usable campaign context.
```

## 9. Approval Gates

The app can prepare real work without silently taking risky actions.

Posting, sending, publishing, spending, uploading, deleting, or changing accounts should stay approval-gated.

This is especially important for managers: the app can move fast without losing control.

## 10. Social Operations

Artist OS Desktop should highlight that agents can help operate social channels, not just write captions.

Key social jobs:

- post or schedule approved content
- manage multiple accounts or profiles per platform
- support artist pages, fan pages, side-project pages, and campaign profiles
- reply to comments
- answer DMs
- prepare dry-run plans before execution
- produce receipts after approved actions

Pitch angle:

```text
Your social worker can help run the channels, not just brainstorm what to post.
```

Important safety rule:

```text
Live posting, replies, DMs, and account changes should be approval-gated and show the exact account/profile before execution.
```

## 11. Manager Multiplication

The app helps one artist or manager operate with the leverage of a larger team.

It does not replace taste, judgment, relationships, or final approval. It compresses the busywork and keeps the operation organized.

Pitch angle:

```text
Move like a bigger team without pretending AI replaces the team.
```

## 12. Human Layer

Some work should go to humans.

Artist OS Desktop can package the right context and create handoff packets for vetted promo providers, expert song review, and future partner workflows.

## 13. Proof Gallery

The pitch should show examples, not only describe features.

Recommended proof items:

- caption pack
- art direction board
- single art prompt/reference packet
- ad plan
- outreach packet
- content calendar
- campaign kit
- final asset slot
- State of Play panel
- worker directory
- multi-profile social posting/reply/DM approval packet
