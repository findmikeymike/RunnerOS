# Landing Page

## Page Job

Make artists and managers understand this is not another chatbot.

It is a music artist command center with specialist workers, living artist context, campaign workspaces, outputs, finals, approvals, and human handoffs.

## Hero

Working headline:

```text
Your music career, in command center form.
```

Support:

```text
Artist OS Desktop gives artists and managers a built-in AI team for releases, content, visuals, outreach, ads, socials, merch, approvals, and campaign execution.
```

CTA ideas:

- Build Your Artist HQ
- Open The Command Center
- Start Your Release Workspace

## Section 1: Why Not Claude, Codex, Or Generic Agent Hubs?

Key points:

- use multiple models and providers
- not locked into one AI company
- interface built for artists and managers
- stocked with music-specific workers, tools, and skills
- designed around campaigns, assets, approvals, and outputs
