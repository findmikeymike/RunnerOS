# Positioning

## Working Category

Music artist command center.

## Working Name

Artist OS Desktop.

This is a placeholder name. Do not treat it as final brand lock.

## Who It Is For

Primary users:

- independent artists
- emerging artists with small teams
- artist managers
- label services operators
- creative directors and campaign leads around artists

## What It Does

Artist OS Desktop helps artists and managers run the work around a music career:

- release planning
- artist positioning
- visual direction
- single/album art direction
- short-form content
- video and clip production
- social publishing prep
- outreach
- ads and paid campaign setup
- playlisting and promo handoffs
- merch and commerce planning
- approvals and final asset organization

## The Main Shift

Generic AI tools make you explain the context every time.

Artist OS Desktop keeps the artist, brand, voice, campaign, audience, assets, and prior decisions alive in the workspace so every agent starts closer to the truth.

## Plain-English Position

It is the AI workbench an artist manager wishes existed: one place to brief the team, launch specialist workers, keep approved campaign assets, and know what should happen next.
