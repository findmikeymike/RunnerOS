# Teams

Teams are durable groups of agents that work under one lead, share a task board, and coordinate through structured internal messages.

## One-line Direction

RunnerOS should not become a chaotic multi-agent chat room. A Team is a reusable operating unit: lead agent, specialist members, mailbox, tasks, wake/resume, verification, and human approval boundaries.
